Install as PWA on Phone or PC

Running this app as a Progressive Web App gives you a native app icon on your home screen, full offline support, and zero data loss on refresh.

Android (Chrome / Edge):

  1. Tap the browser menu (3 dots top-right).
  2. Select "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
  3. Confirm and launch directly from your home screen!

iPhone / iPad (Safari):

  1. Tap the Share button at the bottom navigation bar.
  2. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
  3. Tap Add top-right.

Desktop (Chrome / Safari / Edge):

Click the Install button in the top navigation bar or browser address bar icon.
